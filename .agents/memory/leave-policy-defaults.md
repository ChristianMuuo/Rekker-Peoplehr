name: Leave policy defaults
description: PeopleOS leave policy values and statutory data boundaries.
---

PeopleOS must not hardcode Kenyan statutory leave entitlements. Each organization configures its own leave types, entitlement days, and accrual method.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires organization-configurable policies without statutory entitlement defaults.

**How to apply:** Do not add country-based leave-day defaults or prefilled statutory amounts. Keep entitlement values under organization HR control.
